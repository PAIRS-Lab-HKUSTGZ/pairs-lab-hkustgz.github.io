Moquan Sha is a Research Assistant at PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). He received a double bachelor's degree in Information Engineering and English from Southeast University. His research interests include embodied intelligence, vision-language-action models, long-horizon robotic tasks, failure recovery, and reliable robotic systems.
