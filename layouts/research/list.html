Intelligence Symbiosis

Building a world where people and physical intelligence systems can coexist, collaborate, and co-evolve.

A closed-loop ecosystem

World, people, systems, and data — connected through physical action.

Our long-term vision is an ecosystem in which the real world, people, physical intelligence systems, and data form a reliable closed loop. The world provides the environments, tasks, and physical constraints; people guide, interact with, and evaluate intelligent systems; and robots perceive, reason, act, and learn from feedback in the open world.

Physical intelligence evolves through world perception, human understanding, system decision, autonomous action, and feedback learning. This loop connects real-world experience, human feedback, system execution, and accumulated data.

Ultimately, physical intelligence should make everyday life safer, more capable, and more humane for everyone.
