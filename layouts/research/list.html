Long-term vision

Human–Physical
Intelligence Symbiosis

Building a world where people and physical intelligence systems can coexist, collaborate, and co-evolve.

A closed-loop ecosystem

World, people, systems, and data — one loop.

We study physical intelligence as a living loop: the world provides constraints, people provide goals and feedback, and intelligent systems perceive, decide, act, and learn through real experience.

The aim is simple: make life safer, more capable, and more humane for everyone.
