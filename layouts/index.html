Physical intelligence for the real world.

PAIRS Lab studies perception, learning, and interaction for robots and other intelligent physical systems. We focus on methods that remain reliable outside controlled settings.

Our work is grounded in real systems, public datasets, and reproducible experiments across mobile autonomy, human sensing, and robot manipulation.
